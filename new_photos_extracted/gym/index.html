ABOUT RAISE THE BAR

A Transformation Center Built For Women.

Founded by Mrs. Vaishali Bajpeyi, Raise The Bar Ladies Gym is built on a simple yet powerful belief: Every woman deserves personal attention instead of being treated as just another membership card.

We are not just a gym. We are a supportive community where women in Chhatrapati Sambhajinagar come to reclaim their health, build strength, boost self-confidence, and completely improve their quality of life. Whether you are a busy housewife, a dedicated doctor, a teacher, a corporate professional, or a mother recovering your confidence post-pregnancy, we provide a safe, comfortable, and motivating environment for your fitness journey.

Our Mission

To empower women by offering customized coaching, absolute safety, structured nutrition, and continuous accountability to achieve long-term lifestyle changes.

Our Vision

Become the most trusted ladies-only fitness and transformation brand in Aurangabad, uniting women in health and building life-changing confidence.
